# DinMeter pod on the A-pillar grab-handle cap (JL)

Concept v0. The stock grab-handle bolt cap at the foot of the A-pillar carries a podium and a
1/32 DIN pod for the DinMeter (cutout 45 x 22.5, front wall 2 mm for the stock back clip).

- Base: [Jeep JL A pillar cap (with ball mount)](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4238093) by livesdE,
  file `A_pillar_cap-without_ball.stl`, CC BY-NC-SA. This remix keeps the same license.
- `make_pod.py` builds `apillar_pod.stl`; parameters at the top: PORTRAIT, FACE_YAW, FACE_PITCH, POD_X, LIFT.
- `render.py` makes `apillar_pod.png`.
- Material: ASA (PETG as second choice). PLA softens under the windscreen in summer.
- Rear of the pod is open for the clip; the cable drops through a 9 mm hole into the cap cavity.

Open: which end of the cap points up the pillar, screen yaw/pitch toward the driver, portrait or
landscape, cable route from the cap to the box under the seat.
