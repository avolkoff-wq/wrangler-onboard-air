# DinMeter on the A-pillar grab-handle cap (JL)

Upper red cap of the driver-side grab handle with a wedge and a 1/32 DIN cutout, one printed part.

- `apillar_wedge.stl`: print this. DinMeter long side along the cap, no turn. The upper (wide) end is
  lifted off the pillar by TILT = 20 deg; the wedge flows onto the whole cap outline, front face R3.
- `make_wedge.py`: builds it. Parameters at the top: TILT, POD_X, PLATE size, ROUND.
- `apillar_wedge.png`: preview.
- `A_pillar_cap-without_ball.stl`: base cap, [Jeep JL A pillar cap (with ball mount)](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4238093)
  by livesdE, CC BY-NC-SA. This remix keeps the same license.

Fitting: DinMeter in from the front, stock back clip from inside with the cap off the car, cable through
the cap into the pillar, cap back on. The body goes ~33 mm below the cap face at its lower end: measure the
room behind the cap before printing. Material: ASA (PETG second); PLA softens under the windscreen.

Earlier concepts (podium + sleeve, plain cutout) are in `archive/apillar/`.
